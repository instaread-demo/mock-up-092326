Resident Evil’s Zach Cregger Explains Why 2 Different Versions of the Movie Have Been Released

September 22, 2026

By Christie D'Silva

Listen to audio version of this article

Zach Cregger reveals the reason behind the different versions of his Resident Evil movie. The video game adaptation hit theaters on September 18, 2026, and has received positive reviews from critics and audiences. However, the domestic version of the movie differs from the international one.

Cregger appeared on an episode of The Big Picture podcast, where he revealed that the reboot horror film was finished “maybe” two weeks before release. The Weapons director added that they missed the DCP deadline, resulting in two separate versions for the domestic and international releases.

He said, “It was pencils down at 11:59 on Saturday night because the DCP had to ship. There’s actually… the international version is different than the domestic version because I was still needing to make changes and like we missed our international finish line time, you know? So there are shots I’m not stoked on in the international version that like are better in the domestic. That’s how down to the wire it was.”

However, Cregger did not reveal the differences between the two versions. Additionally, he did not reveal any plans to release the finished domestic version to replace the international one.

What is Zach Cregger’s Resident Evil movie about?

Zach Cregger’s Resident Evil follows Weapons actor Austin Abrams as Bryan, a medical courier who unwittingly finds himself in an action-packed, nonstop race for survival as one fateful, horrifying night collapses into chaos around him.

Although the movie is set in the fictional Raccoon City industrial town from the game, Cregger called it an “original story” not taken from the video game. Like his other acclaimed horror features, Resident Evil received a high score on Rotten Tomatoes. It currently holds a 95% Tomatometer score and a 91% Popcornmeter score on the platform. These numbers also serve as the director’s highest-rated scores on the site.

The movie is co-written by Cregger and Shay Hatten. Apart from Abrams, the ensemble includes Zach Cherry, Kali Reis, Paul Walter Hauser, Will Merrick, Andrea Miltner, and Emily Piggford, among many others. Cregger also produced the movie with Victor Hadida, Robert Kulzer, Roy Lee, Asad Qizilbash, Carter Swan, and Miri Yoon.

Resident Evil is currently out in theaters.
