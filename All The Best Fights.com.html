Isaac Cruz record

The former Wba and current interim Wbc super-Lw champ Isaac Cruz Gonzalez entered this fight with a professional boxing record of 28-3-2 (18 knockouts) and he is ranked as the No.15 light welterweight in the world.
‘Pitbull’ lost to Jose Valenzuela in 2024 (Isaac Cruz Gonzalez vs Jose Valenzuela) but then he has defeated Angel Fierro (Isaac Cruz Gonzalez vs Angel Fierro) and Omar Salcido Gamez; after this bout he has also faced Lamont Roach but it ended in a draw (Isaac Cruz Gonzalez vs Lamont Roach).

 

Nestor Bravo record

His opponent, Nestor Bravo, has an official record of 24-1-0 (17 knockouts) and he entered as the No.83 in the light welterweight division.
He was defeated by Xolisani Ndongeni in 2025 (via split decision) but then he came back to win with Pedro Campa in January 2026.
