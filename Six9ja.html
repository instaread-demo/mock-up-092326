Kizz Daniel is back with another amazing new song “Te amo” giving music lovers another record to vibe with. It has a dynamic sound, and a good vocal delivery that keeps things interesting.

⬇️ DOWNLOAD MP3

“Te amo” is an interesting mix of rhythm, melody and vocals from Kizz Daniel. The song has a good flow to it and keeps the production interesting without taking away from the vocal performance.

The instrumental is a pleasant bed for the record, letting each portion flow together smoothly. A smooth delivery makes it good for a number of listening circumstances.

On “Te amo” the collaboration features Shatta wale on a great vocal performance next to Kizz Daniel. Together they produce a nice record around melody, rhythm and a good delivery.
