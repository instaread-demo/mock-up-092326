LEGO Botanicals 2027 prices rumoured, including tiny $5 flower packs

Build your own bouquet?

Chris Turner-Wharfe · September 21, 2026
Share
X Facebook Reddit
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by

Instaread

Early prices for next year’s rumoured LEGO Botanicals sets have landed online, including for the tiny 70-piece flower sets.

Six new flower-themed sets are said to be dropping in January, each coming in at under 80 pieces. Brick Clicker now reports (via jjpleaksite) that they’ll retail for $4.99 apiece in the US. That’s on a par with the price of a single Collectible Minifigures box, and suggests these sets – which are slated to include 11533 Malva Flowers and 11534 Bluebell Flowers – will act as Botanicals-themed impulse purchases.

Given their size, they’ll hopefully also be a nice way to curate your own bouquet, buying individual flowers to your tastes rather than the preselected assortments of the larger LEGO Botanicals bouquets. We’re only speculating based on the set names and prices there, though.

The same source has also published prices for three previously-rumoured LEGO Botanicals sets said to be coming in January. 11515 Peony Bouquet is said to retail for $59.99 for 633 pieces, 11516 Cherry Blossom Branches will reportedly cost $29.99 for 405 pieces, and 11518 Plants in Love – previously thought to be named Lovely Plants – is rumoured to cost $22.99 for 274 pieces.

That would put the latter set at the same price as November’s 10371 Cosy Plants, so it wouldn’t be surprising to see it built in the same style (see also: 10349 Happy Plants and 11506 Rocking Plants). We’ll have to wait for the LEGO Group to officially unveil its next wave of Botanicals sets to find out for sure – in the meantime, take these rumours with the customary pinch of salt…
