Miley Cyrus’ ‘Innocent Ways’ Fuels Noah Cyrus Speculation With Emotional Sister Lyrics

Miley Cyrus is getting intensely personal on her new album, and one song has listeners wondering whether she is addressing her relationship with younger sister Noah Cyrus.

"Innocent Ways," a track from Miley's newly released 10th studio album, Bass Persuades, centers on an older sister worried about a sibling going through a difficult period. The narrator describes late-night calls, emotional turmoil and unsuccessful attempts to reach the person she loves.

Entertainment Weekly and other outlets have interpreted the song as seemingly being about Noah, but Miley has not publicly identified her sister as the track's subject.

That distinction matters, particularly given years of public speculation about relationships within the Cyrus family.

Miley Cyrus Sings About a Sister 'in a Crisis'

The opening of "Innocent Ways" leaves little doubt that Miley is writing about a sister, although it does not name Noah.

The song describes wanting something better for that person while watching her struggle through what Miley calls a "crisis." Later, the narrator regrets sending an emotionally charged letter and worries after several phone calls go unanswered.

The chorus shifts toward the perspective Miley has gained with age. One of its central ideas is that "grace comes with age" — a theme she also discussed directly with Zane Lowe around the album's release.

Miley told Lowe that younger people cannot always be persuaded that time will change their understanding of an experience. She also reflected on how adulthood has altered her own ideas about success, maturity and what matters to her.

Those comments establish the song's broader themes. They do not, however, confirm the identity of the sister in the lyrics.

Noah, 26, is Miley's younger sister, which naturally makes her the focus of listener speculation. But without confirmation from Miley, describing "Innocent Ways" as definitively about Noah would go further than the available evidence.

Noah Cyrus Has Addressed the Family-Drama Narrative

The speculation also lands against years of headlines surrounding the Cyrus family following Billy Ray Cyrus and Tish Cyrus' divorce.

Noah has previously pushed back against the idea that public reports fully capture what happens inside the family.

In a 2025 interview with People, she said she tries not to become consumed by outside narratives about her relatives and emphasized that music continues to connect the family. She described the Cyruses as close while acknowledging that, like other families, they can experience disagreements.

Noah has also spoken candidly about far more personal struggles.

She has discussed addiction, anxiety and the pressures of growing up beside an internationally famous older sister, while saying that she eventually reached a healthier point in her life. In her People cover interview last year, Noah spoke about recovery, therapy and wanting to continue building a future for herself.

Those disclosures may make some of the imagery in "Innocent Ways" feel particularly suggestive to listeners. They still do not establish that Miley was documenting specific events from Noah's life.

Miley and Noah's Relationship Has Been Scrutinized for Years

Public interest in the sisters' relationship is nothing new.

Noah has previously discussed the difficulty of growing up in Miley's shadow, particularly during the years when Hannah Montana made her older sister one of the most recognizable young stars in entertainment.

At other times, the sisters have publicly supported and performed with each other, complicating any simple narrative that they are permanently estranged.

More recently, Noah emphasized that she prefers not to let public speculation define her family relationships.

That context makes "Innocent Ways" notable without turning it into evidence of a current feud. The song can plausibly be heard as one sister expressing fear, frustration and love toward another; whether those emotions describe Noah specifically remains Miley's story to clarify.

Miley's New Era Includes a Streamlined Name

The song arrives during a broader reinvention for Miley.

For the Bass Persuades era, she has adopted first-name-only "Miley" branding across promotional materials and dropped Cyrus from her Instagram handle. Bass Persuades, released Sept. 18, is her 10th studio album and includes 10 tracks on its standard edition.

Billy Ray publicly supported the branding change, calling it a "natural progression" and recalling that he had always called his daughter simply Miley at home.

There is no evidence that dropping "Cyrus" from her current professional branding was intended as a statement about Noah or a rejection of the wider family.

In fact, Miley has recently spoken publicly about improving family relationships. During the Hannah Montana 20th-anniversary period earlier this year, she said it "feels good" when the Cyruses are getting along and discussed repairing bonds after what she characterized as a difficult stretch for the family.

That makes "Innocent Ways" less convincing as proof of an ongoing feud than as a complicated expression of sisterly concern.

Miley may eventually explain exactly who inspired the song. Until then, its lyrics offer plenty for listeners to interpret — but not enough to turn that interpretation into a confirmed account of what is happening between Miley and Noah Cyrus.
