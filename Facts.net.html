      30 Facts About LSU Basketball

      Ever wondered what makes LSU Basketball so special? From its rich history to standout players, LSU Basketball has a lot to offer. Founded in 1909, the team has grown into a powerhouse in college basketball. With legendary players like Shaquille O'Neal and Pete Maravich, LSU has produced some of the sport's biggest names. The Tigers have made multiple NCAA Tournament appearances, showcasing their talent on a national stage. Their home court, the Pete Maravich Assembly Center, is a fortress where fans gather to cheer on their team. Ready to learn more? Here are 30 facts that will make you appreciate LSU Basketball even more.

      LSU Basketball History

      Louisiana State University (LSU) has a rich basketball history filled with remarkable achievements, legendary players, and unforgettable moments. Here are some fascinating facts about LSU basketball that every fan should know.

      1. 01

        LSU's basketball program began in 1909. The team played its first game against Dixon Academy, winning 35-20.

      2. 02

        The Tigers have made 22 NCAA Tournament appearances. Their first appearance was in 1953.

      3. 03

        LSU reached the Final Four four times. These years were 1953, 1981, 1986, and 2006.

      4. 04

        The Tigers have won 11 SEC regular-season championships. Their first title came in 1935.

      5. 05

        LSU's home games are played at the Pete Maravich Assembly Center. This arena, named after the legendary player, opened in 1972.

      Legendary Players

      LSU has produced some of the most iconic players in basketball history. These athletes have left an indelible mark on the sport.

      1. 06

        Pete Maravich, also known as "Pistol Pete," is one of LSU's most famous players. He still holds the NCAA record for career scoring average with 44.2 points per game.

      2. 07

        Shaquille O'Neal, another LSU legend, played for the Tigers from 1989 to 1992. He went on to have a Hall of Fame NBA career.

      3. 08

        Bob Pettit was LSU's first superstar. He played from 1951 to 1954 and was the first player to score 2,000 points in his career.

      4. 09

        Chris Jackson, later known as Mahmoud Abdul-Rauf, was a scoring sensation. He averaged 29 points per game during his two seasons at LSU.

      5. 10

        Ben Simmons, the first overall pick in the 2016 NBA Draft, played one season at LSU. He averaged 19.2 points, 11.8 rebounds, and 4.8 assists per game.

      Memorable Moments

      LSU basketball has had its share of unforgettable moments that have thrilled fans and made history.

      1. 11

        In 1981, LSU made a Cinderella run to the Final Four. They defeated Wichita State and Arkansas to reach the national semifinals.

      2. 12

        The 1986 team, known as the "Cinderella Tigers," reached the Final Four as an 11-seed. They upset top-seeded Kentucky along the way.

      3. 13

        In 2006, LSU reached the Final Four for the first time in 20 years. They defeated Duke and Texas to get there.

      4. 14

        Pete Maravich scored 69 points in a game against Alabama in 1970. This remains an SEC record.

      5. 15

        Shaquille O'Neal recorded a triple-double with 29 points, 18 rebounds, and 11 blocks against BYU in 1990. This performance is one of the most dominant in LSU history.

      Coaching Legends

      Great coaches have shaped LSU basketball, leading the team to success and developing future stars.

      1. 16

        Dale Brown coached LSU from 1972 to 1997. He is the winningest coach in school history with 448 victories.

      2. 17

        John Brady led LSU to the 2006 Final Four. He coached the Tigers from 1997 to 2008.

      3. 18

        Will Wade, the current head coach, took over in 2017. He has already led the team to multiple NCAA Tournament appearances.

      4. 19

        Harry Rabenhorst was LSU's first great coach. He led the team from 1925 to 1942 and again from 1945 to 1957.

      5. 20

        Press Maravich, Pete Maravich's father, coached LSU from 1966 to 1972. He recruited his son to play for the Tigers.

      Records and Achievements

      LSU basketball has set numerous records and achieved significant milestones over the years.

      1. 21

        LSU holds the record for the most points scored in a single game by a team. They scored 216 points against Southwestern Louisiana in 1989.

      2. 22

        The Tigers have produced 14 NBA first-round draft picks. This includes legends like Shaquille O'Neal and Ben Simmons.

      3. 23

        LSU has had 18 players named All-Americans. Pete Maravich and Shaquille O'Neal are among them.

      4. 24

        The Tigers have won 20 or more games in a season 29 times. This consistency highlights the program's success.

      5. 25

        LSU's longest winning streak is 20 games. This streak occurred during the 1980-81 season.

      Fan Culture

      LSU basketball fans are known for their passion and loyalty. The atmosphere at games is electric, making it a tough place for opponents to play.

      1. 26

        The student section, known as the "Maravich Maniacs," is one of the loudest in college basketball. They create an intimidating environment for visiting teams.

      2. 27

        LSU fans have a tradition of wearing gold to big games. This "Gold Game" tradition unites the crowd and creates a sea of gold in the stands.

      3. 28

        The "Tiger Rag" is the fight song of LSU. It is played at every home game and is a staple of the fan experience.

      4. 29

        LSU's mascot, Mike the Tiger, makes appearances at basketball games. He energizes the crowd and adds to the festive atmosphere.

      5. 30

        The Pete Maravich Assembly Center is often referred to as "The PMAC". It is known for its raucous crowds and home-court advantage.

      Final Whistle on LSU Basketball Facts

      LSU basketball has a rich history filled with legendary players, memorable games, and remarkable achievements. From Shaquille O'Neal's dominance on the court to Pete Maravich's scoring records, the Tigers have left an indelible mark on college basketball. The team's dedication and passion have made them a force to be reckoned with in the NCAA. Whether it's their storied rivalries, iconic moments, or dedicated fan base, LSU basketball continues to captivate and inspire. As the Tigers look to the future, their legacy remains a testament to the spirit and excellence of LSU athletics. Keep these facts in mind next time you catch a game or discuss the history of college basketball. Geaux Tigers!
